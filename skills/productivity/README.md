General workflow skills shipped in the plugin.

## Model-invoked

- **[cv-tailor](./cv-tailor/SKILL.md)**: Tailor a candidate's CV to one job ad from confirmed evidence and prove it with parse, recruiter-search, and blind reader tests through the bundled `cv-tailor` CLI.
- **[imessage-reader](./imessage-reader/SKILL.md)**: Read, search, watch, or explicitly send Apple Messages on macOS through imsg when the user names iMessage, SMS, Messages.app, or Mac message history.
- **[linear-intake](./linear-intake/SKILL.md)**: Capture a sourced idea in a Linear project's Triage, or triage existing intake into research, decisions or deferred work.
